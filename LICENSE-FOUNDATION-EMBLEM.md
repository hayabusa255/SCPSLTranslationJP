# SCP Foundation emblem attribution

The SCP Foundation emblem originates from far2 (based on an Adobe Illustrator
stock asset). Aelanna prepared an early high-resolution PNG. Reference:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SCP_Foundation_(emblem).svg

The emblem is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

The local SVG retains the visual emblem while converting a nested SVG use
reference into direct references, to prevent a missing arrowhead during
rasterization. The 256px PNG is derived from the corrected SVG.

The depicted SCP Foundation organization account is fictional and is not an
actual Entra ID, Windows policy, or organizational enrollment.
